## Scene 3
Visual: Footage of a hiker on a steep trail loop with a heavy pack. Overlay a simple graphic: "1.2-mile loops, 510 feet up and down."
On-screen text: 28 lb day gear, then plus 64 lb simulated load | Empty weight: 5 lb 12.5 oz
Narration: The same reviewer walked one point two mile loops, with five hundred ten feet of climbing and descending. He carried twenty-eight pounds of day gear, then added a sixty-four pound simulated elk quarter. He says the Icon 6 completely remedied the fatigue he'd had with earlier Icons under heavy loads. He also weighed it with the accessories he'd actually use: five pounds, twelve and a half ounces. He calls that in line with the Stone Glacier Sky fifty-nine hundred. So on weight, it's no standout. The difference he describes is in how it carries.

## Scene 4
Visual: Close-up of a GORUCK Rucker 5.0 with a weight plate sliding into the internal plate pocket. Trail footage in the woods.
On-screen text: Source: Ruck Authority, Rucker 5.0 review, at 3:00 | Bag provided free; affiliate links
Narration: Next, the GORUCK Rucker five point oh. Ruck Authority's reviewer says GORUCK sent him the bag, though they didn't tell him what to say. The review also uses affiliate links, so weigh his praise with that in mind. He tested it for a week on Wisconsin trails with a twenty pound plate, and without the optional hip belt. He called it rock solid, with no wobbling. He reported no hot spots, chafing, numbness, or lower back discomfort. His traps were a little sore, and he thought a hip belt might help.

## Scene 5
Visual: Side-by-side comparison graphic of the GORUCK GR1 and the Rucker 5.0, with labels "sternum strap" and "plate pockets."
On-screen text: Source: Ruck Authority, GR1 review, at 2:05 and 3:06
Narration: The same reviewer also tested the GORUCK G R One. At twenty pounds, he says it worked without a chest strap. But he adds that plenty of experienced ruckers will tell you a sternum strap matters as weight goes up, and he isn't arguing with them. His conclusion: for pure rucking, the Rucker five point oh takes the win.
