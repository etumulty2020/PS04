Reptilia Introduces Q-Sert Versions of TORCH Light Body and SOCKET QD Sling Mount

New mounting options bring two established Reptilia accessories to Q-Sert-equipped handguards.

Reptilia announces the launch of the TORCH™ 6V/18650 Q-Sert Light Body and SOCKET™ Q-Sert QD Sling Mount, expanding its accessory lineup with dedicated options for the Q-Sert mounting system.

Both products attach directly to Q-Sert-equipped handguards, providing compact mounting solutions for lights and slings without separate adapters.

TORCH™ 6V/18650 Q-Sert Light Body

The TORCH combines the light body and mounting interface in a single component, keeping the light close to the handguard and reducing unnecessary bulk. Offered in left- and right-side configurations, it allows users to choose the placement that suits their setup.

The TORCH is compatible with Modlite Systems and SureFire® M600DF Scout light heads and tail caps. It is sold as a light body only, not a complete light.

SOCKET™ Q-Sert QD Sling Mount

The SOCKET brings Reptilia’s low-profile, anti-snag sling attachment design to the Q-Sert mounting system. Originally developed in response to an end-user request for a streamlined QD attachment, the SOCKET provides a compact connection point while minimizing protrusions that can catch on equipment.

The new Q-Sert version extends that approach to users seeking a dedicated sling attachment for their Q-Sert-equipped handguards.

MORE INFO

Availability

The TORCH™ 6V/18650 Q-Sert Light Body and SOCKET™ Q-Sert QD Sling Mount are available through Reptilia. For product details and ordering information, visit ReptiliaCorp.com.
