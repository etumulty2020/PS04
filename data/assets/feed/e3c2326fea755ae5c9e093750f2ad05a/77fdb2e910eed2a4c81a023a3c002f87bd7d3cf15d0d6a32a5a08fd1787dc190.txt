Four pounds of glassing gear, a free AI scoring app, and a lightweight rifle built for the mountains. This episode explores gear upgrades that can improve your hunting season. If you hunt the West and want to save weight without losing performance, this conversation is for you.

Host Sam Weaver talks with Brad Carter of Altitude Outdoors about lightweight tripod systems for glassing, shooting, and scouting. Brad explains the Nano Pro fluid head, Mountain SS, and Swift TS tripods. He also shares how tripods can improve shooting positions on steep slopes, in thick brush, and during hunts with new hunters. Brad also introduces Vizirack, his AI-powered scoring and decision-making tool. The app estimates gross scores for elk, mule deer, and whitetails. In addition, it includes Quick Judge, score sheets, trophy-room organization, and hunting hit lists. Brad explains why photo quality matters and how actual score sheets can improve the system over time.

Next, Mike Moore reviews the Sitka Dew Point Ultralight Jacket. He compares the seven-ounce, three-layer shell with the regular Dew Point. He also covers durability, venting, hood design, and wind protection. As a result, listeners can better understand when ultralight rain gear makes sense in the mountains.

Closing out, Jared Bauder of Echelon Rifles discusses custom lightweight rifles, carbon fiber stocks, carbon ARCA rails, and suppressor-friendly barrel lengths. He also explains why steel barrels remain his preference and how barrel coolers can improve zeroing and load development. Talk then shifts towards the other Rokslide.com gear reviews and a recap of the latest Western news.
