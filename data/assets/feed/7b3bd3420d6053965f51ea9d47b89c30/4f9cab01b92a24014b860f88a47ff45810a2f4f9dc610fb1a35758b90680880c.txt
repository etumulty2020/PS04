BargainFinder 577: AccurateShooter’s Deals of the Week

At the request of our readers, we provide select “Deals of the Week”. Every Sunday afternoon or Monday morning we offer our Best Bargain selections. Here are some of the best deals on firearms, hardware, reloading components, optics, and shooting accessories. Be aware that sale prices are subject to change, and once clearance inventory is sold, it’s gone for good. You snooze you lose.

NOTE: All listed products are for sale to persons 18 years of age or older. No products are intended for use by minors.

Powder Valley — Big Powder Sale Plus Discounted HazMat

⏺️ » Great deals on popular powders plus $9.95 HazMat this week

This week Powder Valley is running a notable sale on many powders from Hodgden, Vihtavuori, IMR, and Accurate. You’ll find very good deals on ultra-popular powders including Varget, H4350, H4895, H4831sc and VV N133. In addition to the savings on 1-pound and 8-lb powder jugs, Powder Valley is offering discounted HazMat fees. From October 12 through October 18, Powder Valley is discounting its HazMat Fee to a $9.95 flat rate for all powder orders. That can save you $10-$20.

MidwayUSA — Fall Clearance SALE and Major Discounts

⏺️ » Deals on optics, ammo, hunting gear, plus Free Shipping on $100+ Orders

MidwayUSA is running major discounts in conjunction with hunting season. There are good deals on optics, ammo, hunting accessories and much more. Visit MidwayUSA’s main Sale and Clearance Page to access hundreds of deals with discounts up to 75%. This is a great opportunity if you need trail cams, carry packs, camo clothing and other hunting gear. Plus MidwayUSA is now offering up to 40% off on Leupold Optics.
