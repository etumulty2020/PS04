Introducing Flymotion AirMesh

TAKE MISSION CONNECTIVITY TO THE AIR

FLYMOTION AirMesh™ is a purpose-built UAS connectivity payload designed to extend communications range, expand network coverage, and strengthen connectivity across dynamic operational environments.

Integrated with the Skydio X10D/X10* and powered by Silvus’ battle-proven MANET technology, AirMesh™ provides secure, high-bandwidth connectivity between operators, sensors, and unmanned platforms, supporting critical video, voice, telemetry, and mission data.

Beyond extending operational reach, AirMesh™ can also enable the aircraft to serve as an airborne mesh relay node, helping teams maintain and expand their network where connectivity is needed most.

EXPANDED COVERAGE

EW-RESILIENT CONNECTIVITY

COMMUNICATIONS RELAY

Support the exchange of video, sensor data, telemetry, and voice between connected teams and systems.

Powered by

AirMesh™ integrates the Silvus StreamCaster® LITE 5200 MANET radio, providing secure, high-bandwidth communications and compatibility across the Silvus ecosystem.

Native Integration with

AirMesh™ uses the aircraft’s native payload interface and integrates with supported Ground Control Stations, with no external batteries, wiring, or aircraft modifications required.
