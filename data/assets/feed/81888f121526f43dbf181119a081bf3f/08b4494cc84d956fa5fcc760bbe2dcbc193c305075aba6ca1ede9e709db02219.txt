This episode of Tipsy Tuesday brings together backcountry hunting gear reviews for hunters, backpackers, and outdoor enthusiasts preparing for their next trip. Sam Weaver covers backcountry meals from Peak Refuel, the KUIU Icon 6 pack system, Patagonia’s Nano-Air Ultralight jacket, and a detailed look at Lathrop Super Wides. The episode also includes an update on Colorado’s right-to-hunt-and-fish ballot measure.

Hailey Reeves of Peak Refuel discusses backcountry meals, including calories for weight, water needs, ingredients, protein, shelf life, and meal variety for longer trips. She also talks about favorite meals, limited-run flavors, and the value of finding meals locally before heading out.

Matt Cashell breaks down the KUIU Icon 6 pack system, covering its redesigned frame, load-hauling comfort, lightweight construction, interchangeable bags, fabric durability, and changes from earlier versions.

Mike Moore reviews Patagonia’s Nano-Air Ultralight jacket as a lightweight insulating layer for active use. The discussion covers where it fits in a layering system, how it handles wind and light moisture, and pairing it with a wind shirt for changing mountain conditions.

Robby Denning and Stephen Lathrop discuss mountain boot fit and Lathrop Super Wides. They explain why foot shape matters, how wide and super-wide boots differ, what to look for in heel hold, and how supportive boots can help with comfort and stability over long days in rough terrain.

Finally, Grant Jerry shares an update on Colorado’s right-to-hunt-and-fish ballot measure, including what it would protect, what it would not change, and ways sportsmen and women can stay informed.

Talk then shifts towards the other Rokslide.com gear reviews and a recap of the latest Western news.
