This is a source-based guide to the announced pack, not a hands-on review. For readers choosing a rucking pack now, availability and secure weight carry matter more than preorder excitement.

What GORUCK has announced

GORUCK says it built the M26 on the M23, using feedback from the GORUCK subreddit.

The announced design includes a rain-flapped lid pocket, two side pockets whose volume sits inside the pack, extended main-compartment zipper access, and YKK zippers without AquaGuard. Both sizes have laptop compartments described as fitting a 16-inch MacBook.

Sizes and materials

These are the announced choices in the closed preorder. ROBIC nylon appears in the interior; it is not the advertised exterior fabric.

What remains unverified

The listing does not establish a reliable finished-pack weight or dedicated plate compatibility. Do not infer secure plate retention from laptop fit. Before choosing any pack for weighted training, check the manufacturer's instructions for the specific plate and retention system you intend to use.

Published capacity alone also cannot settle fit. Think about your torso, everyday load, access needs, and whether you will carry a laptop alongside training weight. Those are separate decisions from choosing a color or exterior fabric.

Treat the images and shipping window carefully

GORUCK labels concept imagery as non-final. The illustrations on this article are editorial images, not evidence of finished-product construction or our use of the pack.

For an existing order, follow the seller's order updates and the terms supplied at purchase. A target quarter is useful for planning, but it is not a promise that the pack will arrive before a trip or event. Ask GORUCK directly about cancellation options for your order.

Alternatives to compare while the preorder is closed

Choose an alternative by the job you need it to do. A training pack and an everyday organizer need not solve the same problem. Our existing comparison of Mystery Ranch and other packs can help frame that decision; 5.11 is another brand to compare.

Decide around your next use

If you need a pack for a scheduled trip or a rucking community outing, choose around confirmed availability and your actual load. If your current pack works, a closed preorder is not a reason to replace it.
