Legendary outdoor writer Jim Zumbo has spent more than six decades hunting across the West, writing over 20 books, serving as hunting editor for Outdoor Life, and chasing everything from mule deer and elk to all four species of wild sheep. Jaden Bales caught up with Jim at the Wyoming Wild Sheep Foundation’s Sportsmen’s Expo in Casper, Wyoming, for a conversation packed with hunting stories, hard-earned lessons, and a look at how Western hunting has changed over the years.

Jim takes us back to the glory days of mule deer hunting in the 1960s with greenhorns who wanted to shoot off their horse, recounts a disastrous desert bighorn sheep hunt that ended with his truck burning to the ground, and shares stories from horseback elk hunts and Alaska Dall sheep adventures.

Beyond the stories, we get into old-school still-hunting techniques, finding mule deer in sagebrush country, the evolution of hunting technology, fair-chase ethics, and what today’s hunters can learn from previous generations. Jim also shares his advice for first-time Western hunters, including managing expectations, preparing for the physical demands of elk hunting, and having a solid plan before heading into the mountains.

Originally recorded for the Hunt West Podcast, this episode is being shared on Rokcast while Jaden is out in the field during hunting season. We hope you enjoy!
