Standard plates: one weight for a backpack

GORUCK sells each Standard plate individually. Its current listing gives all three the same 9 × 11.5-inch footprint; the heavier plates get thicker. The exact Standard variant matters: GORUCK also sells a 30 lb Long plate with a different footprint. GORUCK Standard and Long specifications.

That makes the 10, 20 and 30 lb Standard plates the relevant choices for a Basic Rucker or a Standard Ruck Plate Carrier 3.0. GORUCK lists both for this plate format. A shared footprint answers the sizing question; the pocket closure still has to hold the plate properly. Basic Rucker compatibility, RPC compatibility.

Curved plates: two weights and the vest

My Curved sets are the 9.25 lb each and 14.25 lb each versions. There are two plates in a set, one for the front and one for the back. The empty Rucking Weight Vest adds a listed 1.5 lb. Here's the arithmetic:

The 14.25 lb pair was unavailable in GORUCK's October 4 stock check. A lighter pair is also listed: two 6.25 lb plates make a 14 lb setup with the vest. These are the manufacturer weights for the identified variants, rather than new scale measurements. Curved plate options, empty vest weight.

The center opening is the obvious visual difference. GORUCK says the shape is intended to leave room for chest expansion. They aren't a substitute for a Standard backpack plate. GORUCK's design and compatibility notes.

Choose the carrier before the load

For a Basic Rucker or Standard RPC, start with Standard. For the Rucking Weight Vest, price a Curved pair plus the vest. The Achilles has its weight built in, so it doesn't need either purchase.
